### Roman numerals
Convert a given integer, `n`,  to its equivalent roman numerals for `0 < n < 4000`.

|Decimal | 1000 | 900 | 500 | 400 | 100 |90|
|--------|------|-----|-----|-----|----|---|
|Roman | M | CM | D | CD | C |XC|

|Decimal| 50| 40 | 10| 9 | 5 | 4 | 1 |
|-------|---|----|---|---|---|---|---|
|Roman  | L | XL | X | IX| V | IV| I|


### Example
```
input: 4
output = 'IV'
```

```
input: 23
output = 'XXIII'
```
