### Sum right nodes
Given the `root` of a binary tree, return the sum of all the right nodes.

**Example**

* **Input:** `root =  [12, 8, 16, 4, 9, 13, 18, 11]`
```text
                12
               /  \
              8    16
             / \   / \
            4   9 13  18
           /
          11
```
* **Output:** `43`
* **How:** 16 + 9 + 18

**Constraints**

* `0 <= root.length <= 2 * 10^5`
* `root[i]` can be `None` or `-10^5 < root[i] < 10^5`
